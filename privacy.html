Privacy Policy

Last updated July 31, 2026.

Vacation Preview Access is an independent promotional lead service. We collect information you choose to submit, including your name, email address, mobile number, destination preference, travel timing, eligibility responses, preferred callback time, consent records, browser timezone, and campaign parameters.

How information is used

We use submitted information to process your callback request, review potential promotional eligibility, respond to questions, measure campaign performance, and connect you with an authorized vacation representative when appropriate.

Contact consent

When you check the consent box, you agree to receive calls, text messages, and emails about your promotional vacation request. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages.

Information we do not request

Do not submit payment-card numbers, bank information, Social Security numbers, government identification, passwords, or travel loyalty credentials through this website.

Service providers and retention

Information may be transmitted to configured business systems such as a secure webhook, CRM, workflow platform, or email-delivery provider. Records should be retained only as long as needed for the request, legal obligations, consent evidence, and fraud prevention.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of your information and may revoke future marketing contact. Call (813) 524-8915 for assistance.

Important notice

This is not an official hotel, resort, vacation-ownership, or cruise-line website. Submission is a request for contact only and does not confirm eligibility, travel, pricing, or availability.
